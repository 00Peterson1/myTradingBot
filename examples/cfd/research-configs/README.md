# Predeclared starting hypotheses

These six templates declare distinct momentum, breakout or mean-reversion studies by market category. Every symbol receives an independent experiment identity, costs, data checks, holdout and outcome. A template is not evidence of an edge and is not an implementation of an entire cited research paper.

The lookbacks and holding periods count observations and assume minute-resolution exploratory input. Do not use tick-frequency input and describe the same settings as minutes. Forex/index momentum, metal/commodity/crypto breakouts and stock mean reversion are starting hypotheses, not fixed prescriptions for every constituent.

Commission of 3.5 account-currency units per lot per side and two ticks of adverse slippage are **scenario assumptions**, not Deriv fee schedules. Verify or replace them independently for each contract, together with historical financing, effective leverage, lot size and conversion. Use `SCENARIO_BID_ASK` until all those inputs are established. Costs and templates must be predeclared before examining the final holdout. The all-symbol plan does not assert that the Options identifiers map to tradable cTrader CFDs.
